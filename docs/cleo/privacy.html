Privacy policy

Prepared October 30, 2026 · Operator: Carlo Broderick

Scope

Cleo is a private personal assistant for its owner and approved household users. This policy covers the Google-connected assistant and this public information website. It does not grant access to any Google account.

Information accessed and why

Depending on the connected account and permissions granted, Cleo may access account identity, messages and attachments from the assistant inbox, draft content, calendar events and attendees, and Drive files, document contents and spreadsheet data. It uses this information to answer requests, organize records, prepare unsent drafts, manage authorized appointments and work with documents. A separate authorized Drive backup process can upload retained workspace records.

The owner's Gmail drafting permission is distinct from the assistant inbox's read-only permission. Draft preparation does not authorize sending email. Calendar changes and document operations are limited by user authorization and the permissions granted to the connection.

Storage and retention

Relevant messages, attachments, summaries and operational records may be retained in the operator's private workspace, local service records and backups, including an authorized Google Drive backup. Google authorization credentials are kept in protected credential storage, not in these public website files. There is no single automatic deletion period for all assistant records: retained records remain until removed by the operator or the relevant backup-retention process.

Processing and sharing

To provide requested assistant features, relevant information may be processed by configured AI model providers and infrastructure services. Google processes data for the connected Google services; GitHub hosts this public information site. Provider handling and retention depend on the service and account settings in use. Data processing is not limited to the operator's computer, and this policy does not promise that providers retain no data.

Cleo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google API data is permitted to be processed only for the disclosed user-facing features and applicable Limited Use exceptions, not sold, used for advertising, or used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Any provider receiving Google API data must be configured and used consistently with those restrictions.

Access and security

Access to the private assistant is restricted to authorized users and its operator. Service providers may process information as needed to operate requested features. Account permissions, protected credentials and private-access controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Private account records are not published to this website.

Disconnecting and deleting information

You can revoke Google access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously saved records. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of retained information, contact cleo.broderick.ai@gmail.com. The operator handles requests for local records and available backup copies; provider-controlled records and backups are subject to their own deletion processes.

This public website

These static pages contain no signup form, Google login, embedded third-party analytics or application database. GitHub may process ordinary website requests and technical logs under its privacy statement.

Changes and contact

The operator will update this page when the disclosed practices change. Questions: cleo.broderick.ai@gmail.com.
