Research Associate · NCEAS, UC Santa Barbara

Carlo Broderick

I'm an environmental data scientist at the National Center for Ecological Analysis and Synthesis (NCEAS). I work on the Wildfire Resilience Index, building the geospatial pipelines and interactive maps that show where communities are exposed to wildfire and how well they can respond.

In 2025–26 I was a Fulbright Scholar in Asunción, Paraguay, and I still work with partners there on fire simulation. Before that I was an Ocean Health Index fellow, and I spent several years in supply-chain sustainability, starting as an early employee at SupplyShift.

Selected work

Wildfire, ocean health and open tools for data science.

NCEAS · 2023–present

Wildfire Resilience Index

An index of how well communities in the western U.S. can resist, recover from and adapt to wildfire. I process and analyse the data behind its indicators, from road networks to burn hazard.

Interactive map · 2026

Wildfire evacuation risk across U.S. communities

How many roads lead out of 30,728 small U.S. communities, set against how likely the land around them is to burn. Built from OpenStreetMap road networks and national burn-probability data.

Paraguay · 2025–present

Parafire

A fire-risk, simulation and rapid-response platform for INFONA, Paraguay's national forestry institute, with Forest4Life Paraguay. It includes a review of the country's weather-station data and a fire-spread simulator tested against historical fires.
